EVENT BRIEFING // LEVEL 02

OVERCLOCK at a Glance

OVERCLOCK 2027 is a 24-hour on-campus physical hardware engineering hackathon jointly organised by EESA, ISLE, and the IEEE Student Branch at Fr. C. Rodrigues Institute of Technology (FCRIT), Vashi. Emerging builders are invited to tackle rigorous technical challenges, engineer working circuits, and deploy physical systems under relentless clock cycles.

MEMO 01 // OVERVIEW

WHAT IT IS

A 24-hour physical hardware engineering hackathon where teams wire, solder, code, and deploy working electronic prototypes and embedded hardware, not just presentation decks.

MEMO 02 // PERSONNEL

WHO CAN JOIN

Open to undergraduate and polytechnic engineering students. Team composition parameters and eligibility guidelines will be detailed in the official rulebook upon registration release.

MEMO 03 // LOGISTICS

WHAT YOU GET

Full access to campus testing laboratories, digital oscilloscopes, bench power supplies, technical guidance from faculty and IEEE mentors, and official institutional certificates.

MEMO 04 // RATIONALE

WHY HARDWARE

Real discrete circuitry, physical transducers, real-time microcontrollers, and oscilloscope debugging. Build tangible engineering solutions that interact with the physical world.

EVALUATION PROTOCOL // LEVEL 08

Judging and Recognition

Rigorous, multi-faceted hardware evaluation protocol designed to test schematic precision, embedded execution, and functional reliability.

PROVISIONAL EVALUATION BENCH SUBJECT TO FORMAL RATIFICATION

Preliminary briefing notes specify a 3-judge evaluation bench consisting of 2 Internal Judges (academic faculty) and 1 External Judge (industry hardware specialist). Formal appointments will be announced prior to the finals.

PILLAR 01

Hardware Architecture

Circuit schematic integrity, component selection, board layout safety, power dissipation, and discrete signal cleanliness.

PILLAR 02

Embedded Firmware

Real-time controller responsiveness, sensor calibration, communication bus reliability (I2C/SPI/UART), and bug-free execution.

PILLAR 03

Problem Alignment

Novelty and technical depth applied to the selected domain challenge statement, with feasible real-world utility.

PILLAR 04

Physical Working Demo

Live on-bench functional demonstration of the assembled physical prototype operating under evaluation test cycles.

INQUIRY ARCHIVE // LEVEL 10

Case Files (FAQ)

Classified inquiries and operational questions answered directly by the Bureau.

CASE #001 Who is organizing OVERCLOCK 2027?
[OPEN]
ANSWERED
OVERCLOCK 2027 is organized by the IEEE Student Branch at Fr. C. Rodrigues Institute of Technology (FCRIT), Vashi, Navi Mumbai.
CASE #002 When does registration open and what is the fee?
[EXPAND]
ANSWERED
Registration opens on 01 November 2026. The listed registration fee is ₹1,500 + 18% GST (approx. ₹1,770 total). Whether this fee applies per team or per participant will be confirmed upon registration portal launch.
CASE #003 What is the problem statement and submission schedule?
[EXPAND]
ANSWERED
Initial problem statements will be released on 01 December 2026. Teams submit their preliminary approach during the PPT submission window from 10–15 December 2026. Shortlisted teams are announced on 22 December 2026, and the final problem statement releases on 22 January 2027.
CASE #004 Is on-campus accommodation provided?
[EXPAND]
ANSWERED
Yes. Accommodation on campus is available at nominal cost on a first-come, first-served basis. Approximately 100 beds are available overall, with a standard guideline of two beds per team.
CASE #005 Can we submit software-only projects?
[EXPAND]
ANSWERED
No. OVERCLOCK is dedicated strictly to physical hardware engineering. Every project must interface with physical electronic hardware, sensors, actuators, or discrete circuitry.
CASE #006 How will projects be evaluated?
[EXPAND]
ANSWERED
Preliminary notes indicate a panel of 2 internal judges and 1 external judge evaluating projects across circuit architecture, firmware reliability, problem statement alignment, and working physical prototype execution.
