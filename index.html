BUREAU OPERATING NOTICE // FLOOR RULES (SUBJECT TO CONFIRMATION)

1. All prototyping must take place physically on campus during the allocated 24-hour cycle.

2. Pre-assembled custom hardware boards are strictly prohibited. Generic breakout boards and modules permitted.

3. Dedicated debugging stations and electrical safety test benches will be staffed continuously.

[EXPAND]
ANSWERED
No. OVERCLOCK is dedicated strictly to physical hardware engineering. Every project must interface with real microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, or discrete circuitry.
