CASE #001 Who is organizing OVERCLOCK 2027?
[OPEN CASE]
ANSWERED
OVERCLOCK 2027 is jointly organized by EESA, ISLE, and the IEEE Student Branch at Fr. C. Rodrigues Institute of Technology (FCRIT), Vashi, Navi Mumbai.
CASE #002 When does registration open and what is the fee?
[OPEN CASE]
ANSWERED
Registration opens on 06 November 2026. Round 1 PPT submission is free. Shortlisted teams advancing to the physical build pay a registration fee of ₹1,500 + 18% GST.
CASE #003 What is the problem statement and submission schedule?
[OPEN CASE]
ANSWERED
Initial problem statements will be released on 01 December 2026. Teams submit their preliminary approach during the PPT submission window from 10–15 December 2026. Shortlisted teams are announced on 22 December 2026, and the final problem statement releases on 22 January 2027.
